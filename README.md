Game Overview


Mc: The Browser Game is a browser-based online game where players take on the role of Mickey Mouse, navigating through levels to collect coins while avoiding obstacles.



How to Play


To play Mc, simply navigate to the website and start exploring the various levels. Your goal is to collect as many coins as possible while avoiding enemies such as Goofy and Donald Duck.



Controls


The game uses standard mouse controls: left-click to move Mickey, right-click to jump, and space bar to pause the game.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Coin Collecting




Here are some tips for collecting coins effectively:



    
Use your mouse to hover over enemies and then click on them to attack.

    
Mickey's size increases as you collect more coins, allowing him to reach higher levels and avoid obstacles.

    
Save your progress regularly to avoid losing coins or progress in case of a crash.
